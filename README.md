# PitWall Driver

The Windows app for PitWall drivers: it sends your Assetto Corsa car to your team's pit wall and carries the team radio.

**[Download PitWall-Driver.exe](https://github.com/claver-tech/pitwall-driver/releases/latest/download/PitWall-Driver.exe)** (Windows 10/11, about 65 MB, nothing to install)

Then follow the setup guide: **https://pitwall.clavertech.ca/setup.html**

1. Double-click `PitWall-Driver.exe`. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the app isn't signed by a big company).
2. Your browser opens the PitWall Driver page. Paste the invite code your engineer sent you, choose your push-to-talk button, and click **Install** and **Turn on** for the Assetto Corsa app.
3. Keep the small black window open while you race.

This repository only hosts the downloads.
